How a conversation actually flows

Four steps, most conversations stop at step two.

01

Describe it, or show it

Type the issue in any language, or attach a screenshot. The assistant reads both — error codes, amounts, screen names included.

02

Resolved in the chat

Payment status checks, refund timelines, account questions — handled directly, with steps when there are steps to give.

03

Ticket drafted

When something genuinely needs a person — an override, a suspected fraud case, a stuck payment — a clean summary gets written automatically.

04

Routed for real

The ticket goes to your support inbox and Telegram ops channel. Payment-gateway issues also loop in Razorpay support directly.
